My Favorite Series: **Our Beloved Summer**

My favorite Korean drama is **Our Beloved Summer**. It is a *romantic comedy and coming-of-age* series starring Choi Woo-shik and Kim Da-mi.

The story follows two former lovers who unexpectedly reunite when a documentary they filmed during high school becomes popular again.


## Main Characters

- **Choi Ung** – A talented illustrator who loves a peaceful life.
- **Kook Yeon-su** – An ambitious and hardworking woman.
- **Kim Ji-ung** – A documentary director and their mutual friend.
- **NJ** – A famous K-pop idol who becomes interested in Choi Ung.

### My Favorite Characters (Top 3)

1. Choi Ung
2. Kook Yeon-su
3. Kim Ji-ung

## Why I Love Our Beloved Summer

I love this series because:

- It has a beautiful and realistic love story.
- The characters have amazing chemistry.
- It teaches us about love, forgiveness, and personal growth.
- The cinematography is beautiful.
- The soundtrack makes every scene more emotional.
